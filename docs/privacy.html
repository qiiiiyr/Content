Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This page covers the YouTube and Instagram connections used by the "Cutaway" and "Bongkar Pasang" channels (source code).

What the app does

The app is a private tool. It signs in only to the accounts of its owner, to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's YouTube channels and Instagram accounts, and to read the view counts and other statistics of those same channels and accounts.

What data it handles

  • Only the owner's own channel and account data: video titles and descriptions, view counts, watch-time percentages, subscriber changes and similar statistics.
  • Access keys (OAuth tokens) are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub repository settings and are used only by the owner's automated jobs.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect data about other people or viewers beyond the aggregate numbers YouTube and Instagram provide.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer data to third parties, and it does not use it for advertising.
  • Google user data is used only to provide the features above and is not used to train AI models.

Retention and removal

Statistics are kept as files in the owner's repository. Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google Account security settings or the Instagram and Facebook app settings, and the stored tokens deleted from the repository's secrets.
